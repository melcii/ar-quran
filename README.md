# AR Quran

QR codes on the pages of the Quran that open colorful, low-poly 3D scenes of the events they describe, made for children.

## Projects in this repository

- The AR / QR demo (`web/`, `android/`, `qr/`) lives on the `demo-qr-noah-ark` branch.
- [`storybook/`](storybook/README.md): Quran Storybook, an interactive illustrated companion to the stories of the Quran for children (Expo, iOS / Android / web).
