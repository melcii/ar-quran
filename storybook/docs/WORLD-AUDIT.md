Status columns: **Technical** is Issue Found, Fix Implemented (changed, not re-checked in the browser yet), Retested (the fast validator passes), or Verified (automated) (fast validator and the browser checks pass, in headless Chromium, never on a real phone or by a person). **Content** is Awaiting Content Review for every world until a named scholar reviews it.

## How each world was checked

- **Fast validator** (`npm run worlds:validate`, all 114, about 15 s a world): builds the same land and Rapier collision space as the game, then checks the spawn, every solid from eight sides, slopes, the edge, every footpath walked both ways, that every activity spot can be walked to from the start through story doors (start state and finished state), scenery on activity spots, rocks without colliders, herds, the task graph (prerequisites, loops, story order), ayah references, wording that makes the player a prophet or depicts anyone, and adaptation notes.
- **Browser validator** (`scripts/physics/validate-world.js`): drawn parts without colliders, the same collision checks with the props that only exist when drawn, then live keyboard walking into the nearest solids, the camera from eight angles, explorer scale every frame, and Return to safe ground.
- **Play-through** (`scripts/physics/play-world.js`, selected worlds): every activity finished through the game's own actions, a walking route checked before each move, leave and come back halfway, reload at the end, and Leave this world.
- **Ark** (`ark-checks.js`, `ark-animals.js`): giraffes under the lintel, ramp rails, door while the ramp is up, the floor lifting with the flood, and each animal kind boarded on its own.

## Engine-wide findings

### rocks: Retested
- **Issue:** Rocks between ankle and knee height (0.3 to 0.7 m) were drawn with no collider, so the explorer and animals walked through them. 1 to 14 such rocks in 112 of 114 worlds, and in the Ark meadow.
- **Root cause:** Scenery.place() only gave a collider to rocks with size above 0.6, whatever their height; the missing-collider audit only reads parts drawn under the activity props group, not the instanced rock field.
- **Fix:** Every rock whose drawn top is above 0.28 m is now solid (src/adventure/Scenery.tsx rockTop, src/ark/Scenery.tsx). The world validator checks every placed rock against its collider.

### camera-near: Fix Implemented
- **Issue:** With a wall behind and a roof or branch above, the camera could come as close as 0.25 m to the explorer's head. The lens then sat inside the figure, which looked like the explorer disappearing or being cut open.
- **Root cause:** clampCamera() keeps the camera 0.25 m short of whatever is in the way, with no lower limit relative to the explorer.
- **Fix:** Below 1.2 m the camera now blends smoothly to a view from just above the head wherever there is headroom (Adventure.web.tsx Director). The closest approach is recorded in game.camNear for tests.

### chooser-hud: Retested
- **Issue:** The world list (/adventure, also the fallback when leaving the journal or dressing room) skipped Surah Hud's own world and showed 'Hud: the Ark' as a separate activity in its place.
- **Root cause:** Chooser listed 1-10, then a hand-written Ark card, then 12-114.
- **Fix:** Hud's world is listed like every other; the Ark appears under it as 'Inside Hud: the story of Nuh: the Ark (11:37-44)', from the shared chapter list.

### ark-exit: Fix Implemented
- **Issue:** Leaving the Ark when it was opened directly (a saved link, Continue Adventure after a reload) went to the home screen instead of back to Surah Hud.
- **Root cause:** Fallback route was '/'.
- **Fix:** The fallback is now Surah Hud's world (/adventure/11).

### teleport-blind-spot: Fix Implemented
- **Issue:** The play-through test teleported to every activity, so places no child could walk to still passed (the Ta-Ha sea crossing, a Ta-Ha cairn hill, the At-Tawbah gorge exit for tap-to-go).
- **Root cause:** Test design.
- **Fix:** play-world.js now checks a walking route exists (with the game's own route finder, gates as they are) before every jump, reloads halfway to check story and place are kept, and leaves the world at the end. validate-worlds.mjs floods the walkable ground from the spawn through story doors.

### debug: Fix Implemented
- **Issue:** Only colliders could be shown for debugging.
- **Fix:** ?debug=colliders,nav,triggers,paths (or all): colliders, the route finder's walkable grid round the explorer, every activity spot open now with its use ring, the recipe's footpaths and the explorer's own trail. Development only.
